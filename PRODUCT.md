# Lux Facturas Product Context

## Register

brand

## Product Purpose

Lux Facturas is a premium invoicing and point-of-sale system for Peruvian businesses that want a more complete, careful, and operationally precise alternative to basic invoicing tools.

It combines POS, SUNAT electronic documents, inventory by lots, FIFO stock handling, SIRE exports, WhatsApp sharing, security, guided setup, and optional Apple hardware kits in one polished experience.

## Users

- Peruvian small and mid-sized businesses that sell daily and need reliable tax documents.
- Shops, boutiques, minimarkets, cafes, distributors, and businesses with inventory.
- Owners who want control without adopting a heavy ERP.
- Operators who care about speed at the point of sale and clarity after the sale.
- Accountants or business admins who need clean SUNAT and SIRE workflows.
- Buyers who either already have a compatible Mac or want a complete Apple-based counter kit installed for them.

## Brand Voice

Precise, premium, calm, operational.

The brand should feel like a serious piece of business equipment, not a generic SaaS landing page. It should communicate confidence, carefulness, and a sense that the product was designed by someone who understands both sales counters and SUNAT.

## Strategic Principles

- Lead with operational value, not only electronic invoicing.
- Position as more complete than a basic invoicer and more agile than an ERP.
- Be clear that the product runs on Apple hardware. If the buyer does not have a Mac, sell the complete kit with MacBook, iPad/iPhone, ticket printer, and cash drawer.
- Present Apple hardware as part of the premium operating promise, not as a vague lifestyle badge.
- Use product specificity: POS, SUNAT, FIFO, SIRE, Keychain, reintentos, homologacion, ticket 80mm, WhatsApp.
- Use concrete commercial packages when discussing price: hardware, installation, SUNAT setup, initial product load, training, and monthly support.
- Avoid generic promises like "easy, fast, simple" unless tied to a concrete workflow.
- Prefer demo/access language over mass-market free trial language.

## Anti-References

- Generic dashboard hero with cards and floating metrics.
- Blue SaaS gradient, purple SaaS gradient, beige startup landing.
- "Factura facil y rapido" as the only hook.
- Abstract decorative blobs and icon-card grids as the main visual identity.
- Commodity price-first competitors.

## Competitor Context

Competitors include Wally POS, Bsale Peru, TumiSoft/TumiPOS, Alegra, Susii, NubeFacT, Efact, MiFact, and Bizlinks.

Lux Facturas should not try to look like another cloud invoicing portal. It should feel more premium, more product-led, and more operationally complete.
