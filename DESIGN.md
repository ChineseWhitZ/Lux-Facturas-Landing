# Lux Facturas Design Context

## Design Direction

The landing should feel like a premium operating surface for a serious Peruvian business. Dark, precise, and cinematic, but still readable and trustworthy. The product is not "fun software"; it is the counter, ledger, SUNAT flow, and inventory brain of the business.

Physical scene: a store owner closing a busy day at a Mac on a clean counter, with iPad/iPhone support, ticket printer, SUNAT status, stock, and SIRE without opening five separate tools.

## Color Strategy

Committed dark technical premium.

- Main surface: tinted near-black, never pure black.
- Primary accent: cyan, used for action, focus, and product intelligence.
- Secondary accent: warm gold, used sparingly for premium/operational highlights.
- Supporting accent: mint, used for success/security.
- Light sections: cool frost, not plain white, to give breathing room.

## Typography

Use a confident sans system for now. Avoid generic over-decoration. Large hero type should feel like signage on a piece of professional equipment, not startup marketing.

Rules:

- Strong size contrast between hero and body.
- Body text max line length around 65 to 75 characters.
- No repeated tiny uppercase labels above every section unless they function as navigation markers.
- Avoid negative letter spacing unless absolutely necessary.

## Layout

- First viewport should communicate one clear proposition: installed Apple-based POS, SUNAT invoicing, and hardware kit availability.
- Avoid endless same-size icon cards.
- Use bands with distinct jobs: manifesto, modules, proof, flow, access.
- Asymmetry is welcome, but alignment must remain strict.
- Cards must be compact and purposeful, radius 8px.

## Motion

- Motion should be quiet and functional.
- Button press states should feel responsive.
- Reveals should be short and ease out.
- Avoid bounce, elastic, and excessive scroll theater.

## Components

- Buttons: clear active press feedback.
- Product mockups: should look like a real operating system, not placeholder dashboard art. Use fewer screenshots with more confidence; avoid captions that over-explain that the product is "real".
- Feature modules: fewer, stronger groupings preferred over exhaustive generic grids.
- CTA: "Cotizar kit", "Agendar demo", or "Ver paquetes", not "Probar gratis" as the primary premium action.
